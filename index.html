📋 Scientific Design Guide

Validated design rules from peer-reviewed literature. Every default is based on published specifications.

⫿ Interdigitated Electrode (IDE)

Use: Amperometric/impedimetric biosensors, humidity sensors

  • Finger width: 0.1–2mm (screen print), 25μm (lithography)
  • Gap: ≤ finger width for optimal redox cycling
  • Pairs: 10–50 for adequate signal
  • Bus bar: ≥ 2× finger width for low resistance
  • Pad: ≥ 2mm for reliable wire bonding

Ref: Kosri et al., Nanomaterials 2022 (PMC9741053)

◉ Three-Electrode System

Use: Cyclic voltammetry, impedance spectroscopy, battery testing

  • WE diameter: 1–6mm (3mm standard for CV/EIS)
  • CE area: MUST be ≥ 10× WE area (the "10:1 rule")
  • RE diameter: 1–3mm (Ag/AgCl standard)
  • WE-RE spacing: 2–5mm (closer = less iR drop)

Ref: Ossila "Choosing Working, Reference and Counter Electrodes"

◎ Ring-Disk Electrode

Use: Generator-collector experiments, ORR studies

  • Disk diameter: 3–6mm (5mm Pine Research standard)
  • Gap: 375μm is industry standard for RRDE
  • Ring width: 1–2mm for measurable collection
  • Collection efficiency: N ≈ 0.256 for standard geometry

Ref: Pine Research "Rotating Ring Disk Electrode Fundamentals"

⌇ Serpentine / Meander

Use: Stretchable/wearable electronics, resistive heaters

  • Trace width: 0.5–1mm (screen print)
  • L/H ratio: 1.5–3:1 for optimal stretchability
  • Meander amplitude: ≤ segment_height / 2
  • Waves per segment: 4–12

Ref: Suhaimi et al., Results in Physics 2022

⊞ Electrode Array

Use: High-throughput screening, multiplexed sensing

  • Electrode diameter: 1–5mm (2mm common)
  • Pitch: ≥ 3× diameter to minimise crosstalk
  • Min gap: ≥ 1mm for screen printing
  • Bus bar width: ≥ 1.5mm

Ref: Liu et al., Frontiers in Chemistry 2020

🌀 Spiral Electrode

Use: Impedance sensors, inductors, heating elements

  • Turn spacing: ≥ trace width (no overlap)
  • Turns: 3–10; more = higher impedance
  • Outer radius: ~80% of substrate
  • Inner radius: ≥ 0.5mm for screen printing

Ref: Nature Sci. Reports 2024 (spiral-interdigital electrode)
